# Week 05 — IT Security & Support

## Objective
Triage a synthetic upload support ticket, preserve evidence, carry out an explicitly approved quarantine action and verify that legitimate uploads continue to work.

## Authorized workflow
1. Confirm ticket ownership, account verification and assigned pod against the current server-issued scenario resource.
2. Gather non-secret symptoms, timestamps, upload IDs, service health and relevant SOC incident reference.
3. Identify the approved benign fixture; **do not** open unfamiliar attachments in an unsafe handler.
4. Perform containment only if the mentor has provided an authorized quarantine workflow. Never SSH to the host, manually change volumes, modify database rows or disable security controls.
5. Capture quarantine receipt and confirm that the affected upload can no longer be retrieved by a learner.
6. After the corrected release, verify health and one legitimate upload, then complete the handoff and support ticket.

## Deliverables
- Ticket including customer symptom, scope, ownership and UTC chronology.
- Preserve-before-change evidence, approved change record and quarantine receipt.
- Recovery checks, user-facing summary, escalation and a concise knowledge-base note.

If quarantine tooling is unavailable, stop at triage and escalation; do not invent admin capabilities.
